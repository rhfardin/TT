7. Live transfer to you

The AI closes deals itself. When an owner asks to talk to you, it says "one sec, let me grab him" and rings your phone. You hear who it is and what they want, then press 1 to take the call. If you don't, the AI comes back on and books a callback, so owners never reach your voicemail.

Turn off whenever you can't take calls. It only rings during calling hours.
Calls come from your Twilio number.

8. Booking calls with you (Cal.com)

When an owner wants to talk it through, the AI offers real open times from your Cal.com calendar and books the call during the conversation. Cal.com emails them the invite. Without this, the AI arranges a callback instead.

11. Do-not-call list and daily limit

Numbers on this list are never called, whatever your sheets say. Anyone who asks the AI not to call again is added automatically.

At most this many calls in any 24 hours. A steady, modest pace (about 50–80 per number) helps keep your number from being marked "Spam Likely".
